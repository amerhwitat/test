Network note

This prototype uses WebRTC for direct peer media and a small WebSocket service only for room discovery and connection negotiation. STUN helps peers discover public routes; some networks require a TURN relay. Configure TURN credentials for reliable calls across restrictive NATs. Use HTTPS in deployment—browsers restrict camera and microphone access to secure origins (localhost is allowed).
